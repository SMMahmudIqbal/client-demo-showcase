Questions

Before you ask.

Can I try a demo before we talk?

Yes. That is what this page is for. All 21 restaurant remakes open live from their cards, and so do MATRICE, Bazaar Price Board and CVWala. Try them on your phone first.

Is this your portfolio?

No. This is a client demo page, kept separate on purpose. The full portfolio, with case details for all thirteen builds, lives at smmahmudiqbal.github.io/portfolio.

Do you build for businesses outside food?

Yes. Food is where most of my demos sit, but the builds above include productivity, weather, media and document tools. If your business needs a site or app, ask.

How do we start?

Email me at smmahmudiqbal@gmail.com with what your business does and which demo on this page is closest to what you want. I reply with next steps.

Want a website that works like these do?

Tell me about your business and point at the demo you liked. I will reply with what yours would look like, what it would cost, and how long it would take.
